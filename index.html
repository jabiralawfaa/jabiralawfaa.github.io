07 — Research

Thinking

Academic
direction
Research Topic

Headless Low-Code Web Application Builder.

Status
In progress
Discipline
Software Architecture
A. Research Problem 01

Low-code platforms accelerate delivery but often produce systems that are tightly coupled, opaque, and difficult to extend beyond their original boundaries. The problem is not speed — it is the lack of architectural clarity underneath.

B. Research Question 02

How can a headless low-code web application builder be architected so that its abstraction layers remain composable, replaceable, and decoupled from any specific presentation surface?

C. Research Direction 03

The direction treats the builder as a set of independent layers — modelling, schema, runtime, and rendering — each with explicit contracts. The goal is not to build another platform, but to describe an architecture that other builders could adopt.

  • Layered abstraction model
  • Headless content & schema separation
  • Replaceable rendering surfaces
  • Explicit runtime contracts
